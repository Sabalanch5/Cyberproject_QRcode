What could have happened

It's called "Quishing"

QR code + phishing. A code can hide a malicious link, and you can't read it with your eyes. One scan could:

  • 🔑 Open a fake login page that steals your password
  • 💳 Ask for a "payment" that goes straight to a scammer
  • 📲 Push you to download a malicious app

Where fake codes hide

Look out for them on

Parking meters Flyers & posters "Verify your account" emails Fake delivery notices Restaurant tables Stickers over real codes

Defend yourself

5 rules before you scan

  1. 1
    Preview the linkYour camera shows the address before opening it. Read it. Does the domain match who it claims to be?
  2. 2
    Check for tamperingIs the code a sticker slapped on top of another one? Walk away.
  3. 3
    Be suspicious of urgency"Pay now", "Account locked", "Scan to verify" are pressure tricks. Real organizations rarely ask this way.
  4. 4
    Never log in or pay after a scanType the official website yourself or use the real app instead.
  5. 5
    Turn on MFA & update your phoneEven if a password is stolen, multi-factor authentication can stop the attacker.

Already scanned a bad one?

Do this right away

  • Close the page. Don't enter anything else.
  • Change any password you typed in, and turn on MFA.
  • Call your bank if you entered card details.
  • Report it: your school or work IT team, reportfraud.ftc.gov, or cisa.gov/report.
